**Domain:** `eventrentalcosts.com`  
**Methodology:** Modeled on the Kanha high-performance technical SEO framework, audited via `AgricIDaniel/claude-seo`.

## 1. Core Strategic Thesis

EventRentalCosts.com captures high-intent event equipment rental searches by publishing **real, itemized, vendor-verified pricing** that neither Google Maps local packs nor lead-arbitrage quote aggregators (e.g. Bark, Angi, WeddingWire) disclose upfront.

To conquer the **AdSense "Low-Value Content" gate** and explode organic clicks from 14/mo to 1,000+/mo:
1. **Wave 1 Index Concentration:** Set all 106 thin individual listing pages to `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow" />` and prune from the XML sitemap. Googlebot will only index our 16 high-density comparison pillars and metro hub guides.
2. **Striking-Distance Acceleration:** Upgrade the 4 national guides currently sitting at GSC positions 17–58 with exact-match intent queries, interactive guest estimators, and schema markups to leap directly into the Top 3–5 search positions.
3. **Latent Demand Capture:** Expand metro hub templates to directly answer high-impression queries recorded in GSC (`wedding rentals in [city]`, `catering rental fees in [city]`, `margarita machine rentals`).

## 4. Wave 1 Verification Acceptance Criteria

Every indexable URL must satisfy:
1. **Title Length:** 50–60 characters (Max 62 chars). Must contain `Primary Keyword Hook: Secondary Value | EventRentalCosts`.
2. **Meta Description:** Strictly 120–155 characters with action verb and verified pricing hook.
3. **Information Density:** $\ge 0.60$ on `content_quality.py`.
4. **Repetition Score:** $<30$ (zero `repetitive` flags on `content_quality.py`).
5. **AI Filler Score:** $0/100$ (zero generic phrases or LLM cliches).
6. **Zero Findings:** $0$ Critical, $0$ High, $0$ Medium on `page_checks.py`.
